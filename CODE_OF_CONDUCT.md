# Code of Conduct

CustomCleanTV is an open-source project dedicated to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or technical experience.

## Expected Behavior

- **Be kind and respectful**: We are a community of TV enthusiasts, tinkers, and developers. Treat others with courtesy.
- **Assume good faith**: Understand that bug reports, questions, and PRs come from people trying to improve their TV experience.
- **Provide specifics**: When reporting an issue or behavior on your TV, specify your TV brand, model, Android version, and resolution (e.g., Mi TV 4A, Android 9 Pie, 1280x720).
- **Constructive feedback**: Criticize ideas and code constructively, never individuals.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, hate speech, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing private information (IP addresses, Wi-Fi networks, device serial numbers, ADB keys).
- Malicious PRs or scripts that attempt to brick, damage, or install unauthorized software on user devices.

## Enforcement & Contact

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please reach out to the project maintainer:
- **Maintainer**: [@Loge28](https://github.com/Loge28)
- **Repository**: [github.com/Loge28/CustomCleanTV](https://github.com/Loge28/CustomCleanTV)
- **Reporting**: Open an issue or discussion on the repository.
