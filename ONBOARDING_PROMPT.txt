Key Architectural Context:
1. Entry Point: MainActivity.java manages D-Pad navigation, remote key handling, dynamic popups, and the custom theme/wallpaper pipeline.
2. Theming Engine:
   - RollingClockView.java handles rolling digit clock animations with Dark/White theme support.
   - WhiteLogoDrawable.java adapts popular vector brand logos (YouTube, Netflix, Prime, Spotify, etc.) to white or deep charcoal for contrast against bright custom wallpapers.
   - HiddenRainView.java and ItachiEyesView.java provide optional anime backdrop animations.
3. Wallpaper & Leanback Integration:
   - Leanback Settings and App Info use windowShowWallpaper = true.
   - The launcher continuously mirrors its screen buffer to Android's system WallpaperManager via PixelCopy to prevent pitch-black backgrounds behind side panels.
4. Deployment:
   - Built with Gradle: .\launcher\gradlew.bat -p launcher assembleRelease
   - Deployed over ADB with tools/tv.py via Python.
